# Football Manager V2

A browser-based football management simulation inspired by modern top-level football management games. This project is entirely AI-generated and designed as a Codespace-based prototype to explore how closely an AI can recreate the feel of a deep football management experience.

## Overview

Football Manager V2 is a single-player football management game where you take control of a club and guide it through seasons of competition, strategy, development, recruitment, and financial management. The goal is to build a successful club, develop talent, manage staff, win trophies, and compete against rival managers in a realistic football ecosystem.

This README documents the full intended scope of the game, including the systems and features the project aims to deliver.

## Core Gameplay Pillars

### 1. Club Management
- Build and manage a football club from the ground up or take over an existing team
- Set club vision, financial goals, and long-term strategy
- Manage board expectations, fan sentiment, and media pressure
- Track budget, wage bill, transfer spending, and sponsorship income
- Balance short-term results with long-term sustainability

### 2. Team Selection and Squad Building
- View full squad overview with positions, ratings, contracts, and morale
- Assign players to preferred positions and formations
- Manage captaincy, vice-captaincy, and leadership group
- Compare squad depth across every area of the pitch
- Monitor injured, suspended, and fatigued players
- Make strategic decisions about who needs minutes and who should be sold

### 3. Matchday Experience
- Play matches through simulation or a stylized results engine
- Control tactical setup and player roles before kickoff
- Adjust formations and substitutions during games
- Use pre-match and in-match tactical instructions
- Simulate key match events such as chances, goals, cards, and momentum swings
- Review post-match stats, player ratings, and tactical performance

### 4. Tactical System
- Choose formations such as 4-3-3, 4-2-3-1, 3-5-2, and more
- Set player instructions: pressing, width, aggression, tempo, and defensive line
- Configure roles for defenders, midfielders, and attackers
- Match tactics to opponent styles and league conditions
- Experiment with game plans across domestic and European competitions

### 5. Transfers and Contracts
- Buy and sell players in the transfer market
- Negotiate fees, wages, and contract length
- Evaluate player value based on form, age, potential, and consistency
- Use release clauses, loan options, and buy-back clauses
- Manage squad churn while keeping chemistry and budget under control

### 6. Recruitment and Scouting
- Search for talent locally and internationally
- Filter players by position, age, attributes, value, and potential
- Prioritize youth prospects and high-upside signings
- Track scouting reports and player development trends
- Monitor market demand for key targets

### 7. Youth Academy and Development
- Develop homegrown players through the academy
- Assign youth players to training programs and match minutes
- Track growth curves for technical, physical, and mental attributes
- Promote promising talent to the first team
- Balance academy investment with immediate squad needs

### 8. Training and Performance
- Create specific training routines to improve key attributes
- Focus on fitness, technical ability, tactical understanding, and mentality
- Manage workload to prevent burnout and injuries
- Tailor sessions to squad weaknesses and upcoming opposition
- Track player progression over time

### 9. Staff Management
- Hire and fire coaches, assistant managers, analysts, physiotherapists, and scouts
- Improve recruitment and tactical expertise through staff quality
- Set staff responsibilities across training, analysis, recruitment, and medical care
- Monitor club infrastructure and support network quality

### 10. Finances and Business Operations
- Manage revenue streams: ticket sales, sponsorships, broadcasting deals, and prize money
- Control wage expenditure and transfer budgets
- Invest in stadium upgrades and training facilities
- Improve club commercial value and global brand presence
- Balance spending with long-term financial stability

### 11. League, Cup, and Competition System
- Play through multiple divisions and tournament structures
- Compete in domestic league campaigns and knockout cups
- Earn qualification for European competitions
- Manage fixture congestion and squad rotation
- Pursue league titles, cup glory, and continental success

### 12. World and Player Data Simulation
- Generate realistic player attributes and personality profiles
- Adjust ratings based on consistency, form, age, and role fit
- Track player morale, confidence, and motivation
- Simulate national team call-ups and international recognition
- Evaluate player market value and future resale potential

### 13. AI and Manager Simulation
- Rival clubs with AI-driven decision-making
- Competitive transfer activity from other teams
- AI managers adjusting tactics and squad strategy over time
- Dynamic league standings and unpredictable outcomes
- Realistic pressure on results and squad performance

### 14. Season Progression and Long-Term Strategy
- Simulate seasons with date-based progression
- Manage pre-season planning and mid-season adjustments
- Build a club identity over multiple seasons
- Adapt to changing market conditions and board priorities
- Aim for sustainable success and legacy building

### 15. Save System and Career Mode
- Save progress across seasons and campaigns
- Resume existing saves and continue club development
- Track long-term club history, achievements, and records
- Build a multi-season dynasty or rebuild from lower divisions

## Game Loop

The typical gameplay flow is:

1. Review previous match results and squad performance
2. Manage transfer targets and contract renewals
3. Adjust tactics and training plans
4. Prepare for upcoming fixtures
5. Play or simulate matches
6. Analyze player development and injury reports
7. Plan squad strategy for the next transfer window
8. Repeat across the season
